Low-Cost Multi-Patient Ventilator System (LCMPVS)

Fall 2020 – Spring 2021

Overview

I worked to optimize, design, build, program, and rigorously test a low-cost ventilator system capable of remotely controlling multiple ventilators through a central server. The system uses a Raspberry Pi microprocessor programmed in Java, allowing a single operator to monitor and adjust multiple ventilator units simultaneously.

The video above shows the system running with a single ventilator unit. The project was completed through the Northrop Grumman High School Involvement Partnership (HIP) program, with guidance from three Northrop Grumman engineers.
