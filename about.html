About

Princeton University '26 — B.S.E. Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, Minor in Computer Science

I'm Chimwemwe Chinkuyu, a recent graduate from Princeton University with a degree in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Computer Science. I'm inspired by the possibility to turn ideas into smart, functional solutions at the intersection of hardware and software.

I'm eager to grow in innovation-focused environments and contribute to advanced devices, autonomous systems, and technologies that enhance human health and quality of life. My work spans mechanical design, fabrication, and software development — from building airfoils to writing software for autonomous vehicles.

Outside of engineering, I was a ministry leader in Princeton Christian Fellowship and a member of Princeton's Robotics Club and Princeton Men's Club Soccer team, among other campus involvements, during college. I am an ambassador for Top of the Hills Girls Senior School in Nyahururu, Kenya.
