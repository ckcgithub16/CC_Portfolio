About

Princeton University '26 — B.S.E. Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, Minor in Computer Science

I'm Chimwemwe Chinkuyu, a senior mechanical engineering student at Princeton University with a minor in Computer Science. I'm inspired by the possibility to turn ideas into smart, functional solutions at the intersection of hardware and software.

I'm eager to grow in innovation-focused environments and contribute to advanced devices, autonomous systems, and technologies that enhance human health and quality of life. My work spans mechanical design, fabrication, and software development — from building a load-bearing airfoil to writing autonomous vehicle controllers in Python and ROS.

Outside of engineering, I'm a team leader in Princeton Christian Fellowship, a member of Princeton's Robotics Club and Men's Club Soccer team, and an ambassador for Top of the Hills Girls Senior School in Nyahururu, Kenya.
