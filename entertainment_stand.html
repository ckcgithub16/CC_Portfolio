Minimalist Entertainment Stand

Project Summary

I designed a minimalist entertainment stand to hold a 55-inch television and a PlayStation 5. The structure uses seven wooden boards joined with 30 wooden dowels, with four velvet furniture pads on the bottom for floor protection.

The top and bottom boards are identical, and five perpendicular middle boards create four open cubby holes for storage and the PS5. All parts were modeled in CAD before fabrication.
