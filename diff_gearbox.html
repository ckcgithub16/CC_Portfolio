Custom Differential Gearbox

June 2025

Overview

A custom axle differential gearbox designed entirely in Autodesk Fusion 360. The gearbox uses bevel gears in a planetary differential configuration to distribute drive torque between two output shafts while allowing them to rotate at different speeds, enabling smooth, skid-free turning. Gear geometry was determined using standard bevel gear design formulas applied directly within Fusion 360's parametric modeling environment, driving key parameters such as module, pressure angle, tooth count, and pitch circle diameter through linked equations that update the entire geometry automatically as design inputs change. The full assembly, including the bevel ring gear, spider gears, output half-shafts, hub flanges, and outer casing, was modeled as individual parametric parts within a fully constrained Fusion 360 assembly.
