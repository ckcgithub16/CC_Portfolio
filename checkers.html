Software Development

Checkers Game

April 2022 – May 2022

Overview

This two-player checkers game was built as the final project for Princeton's introductory Java computer science course, in collaboration with a classmate. Both players share the same computer, taking turns clicking to move their pieces.

Rules & Features

  • Standard diagonal movement for all pieces
  • Capture by jumping over opponent pieces
  • Win conditions: capture all opponent pieces, or force a position where the opponent cannot move. If the same board position occurs three times, the player with more pieces wins.
  • King promotion: when a piece reaches the opponent's back row, it becomes a King and can move diagonally in both directions
